1. Introduction & Developer Identification

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for Heo Đất Béo (Package Name: com.satsboy87.heodatbeo), developed and published by SatsBoy87 on the Google Play Store.

We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal financial information. This Privacy Policy details how the application handles data, your choices regarding your data, and our full compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

2. Offline-First Architecture & Data Handling

🔒 Complete Privacy with Offline-First Design
Heo Đất Béo operates on an offline-first architecture. All your financial entries, expenses, budgets, savings goals, and asset logs are stored strictly locally on your own device. We do not operate external database servers, and we never collect, monitor, sell, or share your financial data with any third party.

2.1. Personal Financial & Transaction Data

  • Data types: Income and expense transactions, category budgets, piggy bank disciplined savings targets, gold accumulation records, and streak tracking.
  • Purpose: To record, calculate totals, manage personal budgets, and render visual financial charts for the user.
  • Storage: Stored strictly in local device database storage (SQLite / Local AsyncStorage).

2.2. Google Sign-In & Google Drive Cloud Backup (Optional)

The app provides an optional backup and restore feature using your personal Google Drive:

  • When you voluntarily initiate "Google Drive Backup", the app requests authentication via official Google Sign-In and access to the scoped drive.file / app-specific data folder.
  • Exclusive Purpose: To save and restore your encrypted backup archive file directly between your device and your personal Google Drive account.
  • Guarantee: The app does NOT access, read, or modify any other files, emails, photos, contacts, or documents in your Google account. This process is direct between your device and Google's servers; developer servers are not involved.

2.3. Diagnostics & Crash Reports

The app may receive anonymous crash reports and basic diagnostic performance metrics through default Google Play Console mechanisms to maintain app stability. These reports contain no personally identifiable financial information.

3. Device Permissions Used

The app only requests the minimum necessary permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to establish a direct connection to Google Drive when performing user-initiated cloud backups and restores.
  • Storage / Sharing: Used to export PDF summary reports or backup files when the user explicitly requests to export or share.
  • Notifications: Used to display local reminder notifications for daily expense logging and streak maintenance. You can disable this anytime in your device settings.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Provides subtle haptic feedback when pressing numeric keypad buttons.

4. Third-Party Services

The application integrates solely with trusted Google platform libraries:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Drive API & Google Sign-In (Google API Services User Data Policy)

5. Data Retention & Data Deletion Policy

You maintain complete ownership and control over your data, including the right to delete it at any time:

  • Within the app: You can delete any individual transaction or category, or use the "Clear Data" option in Settings to wipe all records.
  • Via Android Device Settings: Go to Settings > Apps > Heo Đất Béo > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data to permanently erase all locally stored data.
  • Google Drive Backup Deletion: You can open your Google Drive on the web > Settings > Manage Apps > Heo Đất Béo > select Delete hidden app data or disconnect.
  • Assistance & Verification: Since we do not maintain central user databases, uninstalling the app and removing your Google Drive backup permanently destroys 100% of your data. If you have questions regarding data deletion, contact us at quoctuanit87@gmail.com.
